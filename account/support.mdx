Support tickets are the way to reach the people who run your MagicPost site. Each ticket is a conversation you can follow from the Support page.

Open it from **Help → Support** in the sidebar.

## Open a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new ticket">
    On the **Help & Support** page, click **New Ticket**. If you have no tickets yet, click **Create a Ticket** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Describe the problem">
    In **Open a New Ticket**, fill in the **Subject**, **Category**, **Priority** and **Message**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach a screenshot (optional)">
    Use **Attach File** to add a JPG, JPEG or PNG image up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Submit">
    Click **Submit Ticket**. MagicPost opens the new ticket and shows **Your ticket has been submitted.**
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| **Subject** | Required, 3 to 200 characters |
| **Category** | **Technical**, **Billing**, **Account**, **General** or **Feature Request**. The default is **General**. |
| **Priority** | **Low**, **Medium**, **High** or **Urgent**. The default is **Medium**. |
| **Message** | Required, at least 5 characters |
| **Attach File** | Optional, one JPG, JPEG or PNG image, up to 5 MB |

<Tip>
Say what you were doing, what you expected and what happened instead. For a failed post, include the post and channel involved.
</Tip>

Every ticket gets a reference such as `TKT-9F3KQ2M1` (TKT- followed by eight letters and numbers). Quote it when you talk to your administrator.

The form also has an address of its own, `/app/user/support/create`, which you can bookmark.

## Follow your tickets

The Support page lists your tickets, newest first, 10 to a page. Each row shows the subject, priority, reference, category, date, the number of messages and the status.

- The tiles at the top count **All Tickets**, **Open**, **In Progress** and **Resolved**. **Resolved** includes closed tickets.
- The tabs **All**, **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** and **Closed** filter the list.
- **Search tickets...** matches the subject or the reference.

### Ticket statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| **Open** | Your ticket is waiting for a reply from support. |
| **In Progress** | Support is working on it. |
| **Resolved** | Support considers the problem solved. |
| **Closed** | The conversation is finished and read-only. |

Support staff set the status. You cannot close a ticket yourself.

## Reply to a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click the ticket in the list. Messages show in order, with your messages marked **You** and support's marked **Support Team**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write your reply">
    Under **Add a Reply**, type your message. Attach a JPG, JPEG or PNG image up to 5 MB if it helps.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Send Reply**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Attached images open from a **View Attached Image** link in the message.

Replying to a **Resolved** ticket reopens it as **Open**.

<Note>
Check the ticket page for the answer. MagicPost does not email you when support replies.
</Note>

## Tickets that are closed

A closed ticket is read-only. The reply form is replaced with a message that says **This ticket is closed. Open a new ticket if you still need help.** Click **New Ticket** to start a fresh one.
