Your personal settings sit behind the profile block at the bottom of the sidebar. Click your name to open the menu, then pick the page you need.

| Menu entry | Page | Path |
|---|---|---|
| Profile | My Profile | `/app/settings/profile` |
| Security → Password | Password | `/app/settings/password` |
| Security → Two-factor auth | Two-Factor Authentication | `/app/settings/two-factor` |
| Time and date | Time and date | `/app/settings/time` |
| Appearance (top of the menu) | Appearance | `/app/settings/appearance` |

<Note>
The paths are relative to your site address. If you use a language other than the site default, the path starts with that language code.
</Note>

## Update your profile

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Profile">
    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Edit your details">
    In the **Personal information** card, change any of the fields listed below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. A **Saved.** message confirms the update.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Field | Rules |
|---|---|
| Full name | Required, up to 255 characters |
| Email address | Required, must be unique, up to 255 characters |
| Company | Optional, up to 100 characters |
| Phone number | Optional, up to 20 characters |
| Website | Optional, must be a valid URL, up to 255 characters |
| Street address | Optional, up to 255 characters |
| City | Optional, up to 100 characters |
| Postal code | Optional, up to 20 characters |
| Country | Optional, picked from the list |

If billing details are required at checkout, these fields prefill the checkout form. See [Billing and subscription](/account/billing).

### Change your email address

When you save a new email address, MagicPost marks it as unverified. Click **Re-send verification email** under the field and open the link in the message.

<Warning>
Until the address is verified, the workspace pages (dashboard, composer, calendar, channels and the rest) stay closed to you. The settings pages remain available so you can request a new verification email.
</Warning>

### Change your profile photo

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose a file">
    Hover over your photo and click the camera icon, or click the pencil button at the corner of the photo.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save the photo">
    Click **Save photo** at the top of the page. Click **Cancel** to discard the selection.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can upload a JPEG, PNG, WEBP or GIF image up to 4 MB. If you signed up with a social login, the photo from that provider shows until you upload your own. Without a photo, MagicPost shows your initials.

### The Account card

The **Account** card shows your **User ID**, your **Referral ID**, your **Email status** (Verified or Unverified), whether **Two-factor** is on, and your **Member since** date. It also has shortcuts to the **Password** and **Two-factor auth** pages.

When SaaS Business is enabled, the profile page also shows a **Current plan** card with a **Manage subscription** or **View plans** button, and a **Recent billing** card with your five latest orders. See [Billing and subscription](/account/billing).

## Change your password

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Password page">
    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Security** and **Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Enter your **Current password**, then your **New password** and **Confirm Password**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

On a site running in production mode (`APP_ENV=production`), a new password needs at least 12 characters, with upper and lower case letters, a number and a symbol, and it must not appear in known data breaches. On other environments the Laravel default applies, which is a minimum of 8 characters.

If a password check fails, MagicPost clears all three fields so you can start again.

## Two-factor authentication

Two-factor authentication adds a one-time code from an authenticator app (any TOTP app) to every sign-in.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Security** and **Two-factor auth**. If MagicPost shows **Confirm your password**, enter your password and click **Confirm**. A confirmation stays valid for 3 hours by default.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable it">
    Click **Enable 2FA**. The **Enable Two-Factor Authentication** window opens.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the account to your app">
    Scan the QR code with your authenticator app. If you cannot scan, use the setup key under **or, enter the code manually**. Then click **Continue**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with a code">
    On the **Verify Authentication Code** screen, enter the 6-digit code from the app and click **Confirm**. **Back** returns you to the QR code.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Two-factor is only active once you confirm the code. If you close the window before confirming, it stays off and the next visit to the page starts over.

### Recovery codes

Once two-factor is on, the **2FA Recovery Codes** panel appears on the same page.

- **View Recovery Codes** shows your codes and **Hide Recovery Codes** hides them again.
- **Regenerate Codes** creates a new set.
- Each code works once and is removed after use.

Store the codes in a password manager. They are the way back in if you lose your device.

### Sign in with two-factor

After your email and password, MagicPost asks for **Enter your code**. Type the code from your app. If you lost your device, click **Use a recovery code** and enter one of your saved codes.

### Turn two-factor off

On the Two-Factor Authentication page, click **Disable 2FA**. It takes effect immediately, without a further prompt.

## Time and date

This page controls how times read for you across the app. Posts still go out at the moment they were scheduled for.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the page">
    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Time and date**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set your options">
    Choose a time zone, a clock and the first day of the week (described below). The panel at the top, **Right now, for you**, previews the result.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

| Option | Choices |
|---|---|
| **Time zone** | Any time zone in the list. The first entry, **Workspace default**, shows the time zone the administrator set. Click **Use this device's time zone** to pick the one your browser reports. |
| **Clock** | **24-hour** or **12-hour** |
| **Week starts on** | Sunday, Monday or Saturday. The calendar, the dashboard and posting slots use it. |

Until you save a choice, you get the workspace time zone, a 24-hour clock and a Sunday start. The administrator sets the workspace time zone under Admin → Publishing → Settings → **Default timezone**. See [Publishing settings](/admin/publishing-settings).

## Appearance

The user menu has a **Light**, **Dark** and **System** switch. See [Language and theme](/account/language-and-theme).

## Delete your account

<Warning>
Deleting your account permanently deletes the account and its data. This cannot be undone. If you have an active subscription, cancel it from Billing first.
</Warning>

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open My Profile">
    Click your name at the bottom of the sidebar, then click **Profile**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the deletion">
    Scroll to the **Delete account** panel at the bottom of the page and click **Delete account**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Confirm with your password">
    In **Are you sure you want to delete your account?**, enter your **Password** and click **Delete account**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

MagicPost signs you out and returns you to the home page.
