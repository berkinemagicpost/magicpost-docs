---

This page follows the shortest path from upload to a live post. Each step links to the page with the full detail.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Check your hosting">
    Confirm PHP 8.3 or newer, MySQL and the required extensions. See [Requirements](/getting-started/requirements).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the web installer">
    Open `https://YOUR_DOMAIN/install`, follow the six steps, and enter your license key and Envato username when asked. The final screen shows the default admin login and the cron command. See [Web installer](/installation/web-installer).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add the cron job">
    Create a cron job that runs every minute and paste the command from the installer's last screen. Nothing is published until it runs. See [Cron and queue](/installation/cron-and-queue).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Sign in and secure the admin account">
    Open `https://YOUR_DOMAIN/login` and sign in with the default admin account. Then change the email and password right away: open your account menu at the bottom of the sidebar, choose **Profile** to change the email, and **Security → Password** to change the password.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Register one network">
    Pick the network you want to test first and create a developer app for it by following its page under [Publishing channels](/channels/overview). Then in the admin sidebar open **Publishing → Platforms** and do the following:

    1. Click **Add credentials** on the network's card.
    2. Copy the redirect URI shown under **What to register** and add it to the developer app.
    3. Paste the app's ID and secret into the form.
    4. Tick **Available to users** and click **Save**.
    5. Click **Run checks** to confirm the credentials work.

    The card changes to **Live** once the network is configured and enabled.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Connect a channel">
    In the sidebar open **Channels**, click **Add a channel**, choose the network and authorise MagicPost. You return to the **Channels** page with the new channel listed under **Connected accounts**. If the authorisation covers several accounts, pick the ones you want and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Publish a post">
    Open **Composer**, choose the channel, type a caption under **Caption** and click **Publish now**. Confirm in the dialog that opens. To test the scheduler as well, open the **When** tab, choose **At a time**, set a time a few minutes ahead and click **Schedule** instead.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Check the result">
    Open **Posts** to see the post and its status. Then open **Publishing → Settings** in the admin sidebar: **System health** lists anything that still stops posts from going out, and reads **Ready to publish** when nothing does.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
Before you rely on a channel, open **Channels** and click **Check** on its card. Where the network allows it, the **Connection check** offers **Send a private test** so you can confirm posting works without publishing anything public.
</Tip>

<Note>
Email verification, password resets and failed-post alerts need working mail. Configure SMTP under Platform → Backend Settings → SMTP Settings in the admin sidebar. While mail is only written to the log, **System health** reports **Email** as a warning.
</Note>
