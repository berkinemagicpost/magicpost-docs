MagicPost publishes to Facebook **Pages**. Personal profiles and Facebook groups cannot be connected. Each Page you connect is one channel and counts against your plan's channel limit.

For the parts every network shares (credentials, callback URLs, channel health), see [Publishing channels](/channels/overview).

## What you need

- A Meta for Developers account and a Meta app (you create it below).
- Your MagicPost site served over HTTPS. The **Run checks** test on the Platforms page fails the redirect URI check for any address that does not start with `https://`.
- For each person who connects a Page: a role on that Page that includes the `CREATE_CONTENT` task. Pages where the person lacks it are not offered during connection.

## Set up the Meta app

You do this once, as the administrator. The same Meta app can serve Facebook, [Instagram](/channels/meta/instagram) and the [official WhatsApp API](/channels/whatsapp/official-cloud-api).

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the Facebook Page setup panel in MagicPost">
    Go to **Admin → Publishing → Platforms** and click **Add credentials** on the **Facebook Page** card (the button reads **Edit credentials** once keys are saved). The panel shows the **Redirect URI** and the **Permissions asked for**, each with a copy button.

    The redirect URI has this form:

    ```text
    https://your-domain.com/publishing/facebook/callback
    ```

    Copy the exact value from the panel. Networks compare it character for character.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create the app">
    In [Meta for Developers](https://developers.facebook.com/apps), create an app with the use case for managing a Page, and connect it to your business portfolio.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add Facebook Login">
    Add **Facebook Login** to the app and paste the redirect URI under **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs**. If the same app also serves Instagram, add the Instagram redirect URI there as well (`https://your-domain.com/publishing/instagram/callback`).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Request permissions">
    In **App Review**, request advanced access to each permission listed below.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Copy the keys">
    Open **App settings → Basic** and copy the **App ID** and **App Secret**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save them in MagicPost">
    Back in the setup panel, paste the App ID into **Client ID** and the App Secret into **Client secret**. Leave **Custom redirect URI** empty unless your site must be reached at a different address. Tick **Available to users** and click **Save**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Run the checks">
    Click **Run checks** on the card. The **Configuration checks** window confirms the client ID, client secret, redirect URI, requested scopes, connect driver and publisher. Every row should pass.
  </Step>
</Steps>

### Permissions MagicPost requests

The connect dialog asks for these six permissions together:

`pages_show_list`, `pages_read_engagement`, `pages_manage_posts`, `pages_manage_engagement`, `business_management`, `read_insights`

`pages_manage_engagement` is what allows first comments. `read_insights` feeds the Page figures in [Analytics](/user-guide/analytics).

<Warning>
Until App Review approves the permissions, only people who have a role on the Meta app can connect a Page. Meta also asks for a verified business before the app can be published for everyone else.
</Warning>

<Note>
MagicPost calls Graph API version `v23.0`.
</Note>

## Connect a Page

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open Channels">
    In the sidebar, under **Publishing**, click **Channels**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Start the connection">
    In the **Add a channel** card, click **Facebook Page**. If the tile is dashed and marked **Not in your plan**, your plan does not include Facebook. See [Plans](/saas/plans).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Authorise in Facebook">
    Sign in to Facebook, choose the Pages to share and accept every permission. If you decline `pages_manage_engagement`, the first comment field is not offered for that channel.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose Pages">
    If you shared one Page, MagicPost connects it right away and confirms with a message ending in "is connected." If you shared several, the **Choose accounts to connect** window opens with every Page ticked. Untick the ones you do not want and click **Connect**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

When you pick several Pages and your plan's channel limit is reached, the remaining ones are skipped and MagicPost says how many.

If a connection fails, MagicPost returns you to Channels with a message. The ones specific to Facebook:

| Message | Meaning |
|---|---|
| This account does not administer any Facebook pages. | The Facebook account has no Pages. |
| You administer pages but none of them granted permission to publish. | The account has Pages, but none where it holds the `CREATE_CONTENT` task. |
| Meta did not issue a long-lived token, so the connection would stop working within the hour. Try connecting again. | The token swap failed. Nothing was saved. |

## Post types and limits

Choose the type with **Post type** in the **Settings for** panel of the channel in the composer. The limits below are set in `config/publishing.php`, and the composer checks them before a post is queued.

| | Feed post | Reel | Story |
|---|---|---|---|
| Caption | Up to 63,206 characters | Up to 2,200 characters | No caption |
| Media required | No | Yes, one video | Yes, one image or one video |
| Images | 1 to 10 (jpg, jpeg, png, gif, webp), 10 MB each | Not accepted | 1 (jpg, jpeg, png), 10 MB |
| Video | 1 (mp4, mov), up to 1,024 MB, at least 1 second, no maximum length set | 1 (mp4, mov), up to 1,024 MB, 3 to 90 seconds | 1 (mp4, mov), up to 100 MB, 3 to 60 seconds |
| Images and video together | No | Not applicable | No |
| Options | **Link** (a URL of up to 2,048 characters) | None | None |
| First comment | Yes | Yes | No |

### How a post goes out

- **Feed post.** One image is published as a photo. Several images are uploaded unpublished and attached to a single post. A video is handed to Facebook by link when the file's address can be reached from the internet, and uploaded from your server otherwise. A text post can carry a link, taken from the **Link** option or from the shared **Link** field in the **First comment and link** card.
- **Reel.** The video is uploaded through Facebook's resumable upload session, then published. Each step is recorded, so a run that is cut short continues with the same upload.
- **Story.** An image or a video is published as a Page Story.
- **Alt text.** The alt text saved with an image in the Media library is sent with each photo, up to 1,000 characters.

Your server uploads the files itself, so Facebook publishing does not need your media to be reachable from the internet, unlike [Instagram](/channels/meta/instagram) and [Threads](/channels/meta/threads).

## First comments

Feed posts and Reels can carry a first comment, posted as a comment on the post right after it goes live. Write it in the **First comment** field of the **First comment and link** card, or per channel in the **First comment for** field of the channel's settings. A per-channel comment overrides the shared one.

A failed comment does not fail the post. The post stays published and the network's answer is written to the application log under "First comment failed".

## Check the connection

On the Channels page, open the channel's actions menu (the three dots) and click **Check connection**. The **Connection check** window lists:

- **Authorisation**: whether the connection expires and whether it renews itself.
- **Page**: confirms the connection to the Page by name.
- **Visibility**: a warning if the Page is unpublished, because then only its admins see what is posted to it.

The window also offers **Send a private test**. For Facebook this creates an unpublished post that only Page admins can see, then deletes it. If the deletion fails, the message says where to remove the post by hand.

## Analytics

Facebook reports these figures to [Analytics](/user-guide/analytics):

| Level | Figures |
|---|---|
| Post | Likes, comments, shares, clicks, impressions, reach |
| Page | Followers, impressions, reach, engagements |

A Page without the insights permission still reports its follower count.

## Tokens and reconnecting

MagicPost keeps the Page token Meta issues from a long-lived user token. That token has no expiry date of its own and is not renewed on a timer. It stops working when the person revokes the app, loses their role on the Page, or Meta ends the app's data access after a long period without use.

When that happens the channel shows **Needs reconnect**. Open the actions menu and click **Reconnect**. If you pick a different Page than the one you originally linked, MagicPost refuses with "That is a different account. Reconnect using the account you originally linked."

MagicPost also runs the connection check in the background, at most about once a day, for channels that have posts due in the next two days. A revoked token is therefore usually flagged before a scheduled post fails. This needs the scheduler to be running; see [Cron and queue](/installation/cron-and-queue).

## Common errors

<AccordionGroup>
  <Accordion title="Facebook says the redirect URI is not allowed">
    The address in **Valid OAuth Redirect URIs** must match the redirect URI shown in MagicPost exactly, including `https://` and the `/publishing/facebook/callback` path.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="I can connect, but a colleague cannot">
    The app is not yet approved for the permissions. Until App Review passes, only people with a role on the Meta app can connect a Page.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The first comment never appears">
    Check that the connecting person granted `pages_manage_engagement`. Without it the field is not offered. If it was offered and the comment is still missing, look for "First comment failed" in the application log.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="The channel shows Needs reconnect">
    Meta rejected the token (error codes 102, 190, 463 or 467). Reconnect the channel from the actions menu.
  </Accordion>
  <Accordion title="A post failed with a rate limit message">
    Meta throttled the app or the Page. MagicPost retries these automatically on its backoff schedule. See [Failed posts](/troubleshooting/publishing-errors).
  </Accordion>
</AccordionGroup>

More on connection problems: [Channel connection problems](/troubleshooting/channel-connections).
