A plan is what a customer buys. It sets the price, the AI credits, the limits, the features and the networks that customer can use. You manage plans under **Finance** → **Pricing Plans**. This page covers plans of type Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime. Prepaid plans use the same form and are covered in [Prepaid plans](/saas/prepaid-plans).

<Note>
  Plans exist only while [SaaS Business is switched on](/saas/overview). Decide what users get without a plan on the [Free tier](/saas/free-tier) page.
</Note>

## The plans list

Open **Finance** → **Pricing Plans**. The page title is **Plans** and shows the total number of pricing plans.

| Column | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Plan Name | The name customers see. |
| Active Subscribers | How many active subscriptions the plan has. |
| Status | Active, Hidden or Closed. |
| Price | The price with the currency symbol. |
| Plan Type | Monthly, Yearly, Lifetime or Prepaid. |
| Featured | A check mark when the plan is featured. |
| Created | The creation date. |

You can search, sort, filter by **Status**, and show or hide columns. Each row has **Edit** and **Delete** actions. Use the table's reorder control to drag plans into the order you want. Users see plans on their **Billing** page in that order, then by price. The home page pricing section orders active plans by price.

## Create a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Start a new plan">
    Click **Create New Plan** above the table.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Fill in the form">
    Work through the sections described below. Required fields are marked with an asterisk.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Create plan**. You return to the list with the message **Pricing plan created successfully**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

## General

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Plan name | Required. The name subscribers see on the pricing page, at checkout and on their billing page. Between 1 and 255 characters. |
| Description | A short tagline shown with the plan on the pricing, billing and checkout pages. The plan card on the Billing page cuts it at 80 characters. |
| Status | Required. **Active** plans can be bought. **Hidden** plans can only be assigned by an admin. **Closed** plans are no longer offered. |
| Featured | Highlights the plan above the others on the pricing page. Featured plans carry a **Most popular** tag. |

<Note>
  Closing a plan stops new sales. It does not take the plan away from people who already have it.
</Note>

## Pricing

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Plan type | Required. **Monthly** and **Yearly** plans renew automatically each billing cycle. **Lifetime** and **Prepaid** plans are one-time payments with no automatic renewal. |
| Price | Required. What a subscriber pays each billing cycle, or once for Lifetime and Prepaid plans. Zero or more, with two decimals. |
| Currency | Required. The currency subscribers are charged in. Each plan has its own currency. |
| Free plan | Subscribers can sign up to this plan without paying. |
| Free trial days | Subscribers are not charged until the trial days run out. Leave it blank for no trial. Accepts 0 to 65,535. |

<Warning>
  A plan marked **Free plan** is activated from the Billing page without a payment even if its price is above zero. Set **Price** to 0 on free plans.
</Warning>

<Warning>
  Free trial days are passed to Stripe as the trial period of the subscription. The other gateways do not read this field, so a trial is only honored when the customer pays with Stripe.
</Warning>

### How long access lasts

| Plan type | Access period after a payment |
|---|---|
| Monthly | One month. The gateway's renewal charge extends it. |
| Yearly | One year. The gateway's renewal charge extends it. |
| Lifetime | 50 years from the purchase date. |
| Prepaid | Not applicable. Prepaid plans do not create a subscription. |

<Warning>
  Only gateway renewal charges extend a Monthly or Yearly period. A free plan, a plan assigned from [Subscriptions](/saas/subscriptions), and a bank transfer order get a single period. When it ends, the user falls back to the free tier. Choose **Lifetime** for free or assigned plans that should not lapse.
</Warning>

## AI credits

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| AI credits | Required. The credits a subscriber on this plan receives to spend on AI. Accepts -1 to 1,000,000. Use **-1** for unlimited credits. |

Credits on Monthly, Yearly and Lifetime plans are renewed on a monthly basis. Unused monthly allowance does not carry over. Credits are spent on AI writing and AI media runs, at the cost per run set on the **Writing Models** and **Media Models** pages. See [AI models](/admin/ai-models) and [Credits and allowances](/saas/credits).

## AI features

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| AI writing | Lets subscribers write, rewrite and translate captions with AI and use hashtag and alt-text suggestions and brand voices. |
| AI media | Lets subscribers generate images and videos with AI in the Media Studio. |

## Publishing limits

Each limit has a number box and an **Unlimited** switch. The text next to the box previews the result, for example **Unlimited** or **Not included**. A value of 0 means the plan does not include that capability.

| Limit | What it counts | Highest value |
|---|---|---|
| Connected channels | How many social accounts a subscriber can have connected at once. | 1,000 |
| Posts per month | Posts created in the current calendar month. A post that went out still counts after it is deleted. Recurring series count by their occurrences, not by the series itself. | 1,000,000 |
| Posts scheduled ahead | Posts that are scheduled, waiting for approval or paused at the same time. | 100,000 |
| Media storage | How much space the files in the subscriber's media library can take up. Pick **MB** or **GB** next to the box. | 10,485,760 MB, which is 10,240 GB |

## Workspace and team

| Field | What it does |
|---|---|
| Team seats | How many members a subscriber can add to their team, not counting themselves. Highest value 1,000. At 0 the Teams feature is not available on the plan. |
| Approval workflow | Posts by a subscriber's team members wait for the owner's approval before they are scheduled. |

See [Teams](/account/teams) for how owners and members use this.

## Networks

**Allowed networks** controls which social networks subscribers can use. Subscribers can use only the ticked networks, or every network enabled on the **Platforms** page when none is ticked. A network that you later disable on that page stays listed with the note **Disabled on the Platforms page**. See [Channel credentials](/admin/publishing-platforms).

## Feature access

| Switch | What it does |
|---|---|
| Analytics | Opens the Analytics page, the analytics panels on the dashboard and the analytics API endpoint. |
| API and webhooks | Lets subscribers create API keys and webhook endpoints and use the REST API. |
| RSS feeds | Lets subscribers add RSS feeds whose new items become posts. |
| Bulk CSV import | Lets subscribers create many posts at once by importing a CSV file. |
| Recurring and evergreen posts | Lets subscribers repeat posts on a schedule and recycle evergreen posts. |
| Watermark-free | Images from subscribers on this plan go out without the watermark set in Publishing Settings. Takes effect once a watermark is set in [Publishing settings](/admin/publishing-settings). |

## Custom features

Custom features are extra perks listed with the plan on the pricing page. They grant nothing on their own.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Type the perk">
    Enter text in **Custom feature**, for example a support promise.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add it">
    Click **Add** or press Enter. Each entry appears below as a tag with a remove button.
  </Step>
</Steps>

You can add up to 50 custom features of up to 255 characters each. Duplicates are ignored, whatever their capitalization.

## Limits that plans do not control

A few caps are fixed for every user, whatever plan they have.

| Item | Cap per user |
|---|---|
| RSS feeds | 20 |
| API keys | 10 |
| Webhook endpoints | 10 |
| Brand voices | 20 |
| Rows read from one CSV import | 200 |

## Edit a plan

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the plan">
    On the plans list, open the actions for the plan and choose **Edit**. The plan's ID appears under the heading.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Change the fields">
    The form is the same as for a new plan.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save changes**. The message **Pricing plan updated successfully** confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Limits, features, networks and credits are read from the plan whenever MagicPost checks what a subscriber may do. A change therefore applies to every current subscriber straight away.

If your change makes a plan stricter, a **Tighten this plan?** dialog opens first. It shows how many subscribers are affected and lists the controls that get tighter: a lower limit, a feature switched off, or fewer allowed networks. Existing work keeps running and nothing is deleted. A subscriber who is already over a lowered limit can add no more of that kind until usage falls below it. Click **Save anyway** to continue or **Cancel** to go back. The dialog does not appear when nobody is on the plan.

## Gateway plan IDs

The plan form has no fields for gateway plan or product IDs. MagicPost creates the matching product, price or plan at the payment gateway itself.

- The first time a customer starts a Monthly or Yearly subscription through a gateway, MagicPost creates the gateway-side plan and stores its ID.
- If you change the plan's name, price, currency or type, the next checkout creates a fresh price at the gateway. On Stripe the old price is archived first.
- Which gateways appear at checkout depends on the plan type. Monthly and Yearly plans use gateways enabled for subscriptions. Lifetime and Prepaid plans use gateways enabled for one-time plans. See [Payment gateways](/gateways/overview).

To create or refresh gateway plans ahead of time, run the sync command on the server. It covers active plans with a price above zero.

<CodeGroup>
```bash All gateways
php artisan payments:sync-plans
```

```bash One gateway
php artisan payments:sync-plans --gateway=stripe
```

```bash One gateway and one plan
php artisan payments:sync-plans --gateway=paypal --plan=PLAN_ID
```
</CodeGroup>

Replace `PLAN_ID` with the ID shown on the **Edit Plan** page. The command prints the gateway-side ID for each plan, or the error when a gateway fails. Stripe and Paddle are synced for every paid plan, including Lifetime and Prepaid. The other gateways are synced for Monthly and Yearly plans only.

## Delete a plan

Open the row actions and choose **Delete**, then confirm. A plan that has subscription records cannot be deleted, because the database blocks it. Set its **Status** to **Closed** instead.

## What customers see

Active plans appear on the home page pricing section and on the **Billing** page, grouped by plan type. The plan card lists a **What's included** summary built from the plan: credits, every limit above 0, every feature that is on, the allowed networks and your custom features. A plan with **Free plan** on or a price of 0 is shown with a **Free** tag and the **Get started** button.
