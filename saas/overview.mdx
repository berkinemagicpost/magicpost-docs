MagicPost can run as a paid service. Billing ships as the **SaaS Business** plugin. When the plugin is installed and switched on, you can sell subscription plans and prepaid credit packs, take payments through the gateways you configure, issue invoices and run a referral program. When it is off, MagicPost has no plan limits at all.

## What SaaS Business adds

The plugin adds two sections to the admin sidebar and two pages to the user sidebar.

| Where | Menu item | What it is for | Guide |
|---|---|---|---|
| Admin, **Finance** | Finance Dashboard | Revenue, orders, subscriptions and gateway results | [Finance dashboard](/saas/finance-dashboard) |
| Admin, **Finance** | Pricing Plans | Create and edit plans | [Subscription plans](/saas/plans), [Prepaid plans](/saas/prepaid-plans) |
| Admin, **Finance** | Free tier | Limits for users without a plan | [Free tier](/saas/free-tier) |
| Admin, **Finance** | Orders | Every payment, bank transfer approvals | [Orders and transactions](/saas/orders) |
| Admin, **Finance** | Subscriptions | Who is subscribed to what | [Subscriptions](/saas/subscriptions) |
| Admin, **Finance** | Invoice Settings | Seller details printed on invoices | [Invoices](/saas/invoices) |
| Admin, **Finance** | Payment Gateways | Stripe, PayPal and the others | [Payment gateways](/gateways/overview) |
| Admin, **Finance** | Finance Settings | Currency, tax and billing details | [Finance settings](/saas/finance-settings) |
| Admin, **Affiliates** | Accounts, Earnings, Payouts | The referral program | [Referral program admin](/saas/affiliates) |
| User, **Plans** | Billing, Referrals | Plan selection, billing history, referral link | [Billing and subscription](/account/billing), [Referrals](/account/referrals) |

The public home page also gets a **Pricing** section that lists your active plans. Credits are covered in [Credits and allowances](/saas/credits).

## Turn on SaaS Business

<Steps>
  <Step title="Install the plugin">
    Open **Plugins** in the **Platform** section of the admin sidebar and find **SaaS Business**. If the card does not show **Installed**, click **Install**. Depending on the listing and your license, the button reads **Install**, **Install · Extended** or **Buy Now**. See [Plugins and themes](/admin/plugins-and-themes).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Open the plugin settings">
    Go to **General Settings** in the **Platform** section, open the **Plugins** tab and click the **SaaS Business** card. The card only appears once the plugin is installed.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable billing">
    Switch on **Enable SaaS Business** and click **Save**. The switch description reads: when enabled, billing, plans, subscriptions and referral features become available across the platform.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Reload the admin panel">
    The **Finance** and **Affiliates** sections now appear in the sidebar, and users see **Billing** and **Referrals** under **Plans**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  Both conditions must hold. The sidebar sections, the pricing section and the billing pages show only when the plugin is installed and **Enable SaaS Business** is on.
</Note>

## What changes for your users

When billing is on, MagicPost decides what each person may do from the first rule that matches.

| Who | What applies |
|---|---|
| An admin account | No limits, every feature on. |
| A user with an active Monthly, Yearly or Lifetime subscription | The limits, features and networks of that plan. If a user has more than one active subscription, the most recently started one applies. |
| Everyone else: no subscription, an expired or cancelled one, or only a prepaid pack | The [free tier](/saas/free-tier). |

Prepaid plans never change limits or features. They only add credits. See [Prepaid plans](/saas/prepaid-plans).

When billing is off, nobody is limited: every limit is unlimited and every feature is on, except **Watermark-free**, which stays off.

<Warning>
  Switching **Enable SaaS Business** off does not delete plans, orders or subscriptions, but it removes every limit for every user, paying or not. Turn it off only when you really want an unrestricted install.
</Warning>

## Plan types

| Type | How it is paid | Credits |
|---|---|---|
| Monthly | Renews every month | The allowance is renewed each month |
| Yearly | Renews every year | The allowance is renewed each month |
| Lifetime | One payment | The allowance is renewed each month |
| Prepaid | One payment | Added once to a separate balance and kept until used |

## Recommended setup order

<Steps>
  <Step title="Set the currency and tax">
    Open **Finance Settings** and choose the default currency, tax rate and whether checkout asks for billing details. See [Finance settings](/saas/finance-settings).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add your company details">
    Fill in **Invoice Settings** so invoices carry your business name and address. See [Invoices](/saas/invoices).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable a payment gateway">
    Configure at least one gateway under **Payment Gateways**. Start with [Payment gateways](/gateways/overview).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the free tier">
    Decide what users get before they pay. A new install allows 0 connected channels, so users without a plan cannot connect any account until you raise it. See [Free tier](/saas/free-tier).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Create your plans">
    Add the subscription and prepaid plans you want to sell under **Pricing Plans**. See [Subscription plans](/saas/plans).
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the registration credits and AI costs">
    Choose how many credits new users receive and what each AI run costs. See [Credits and allowances](/saas/credits).
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Credits, allowance refreshes and expired holds depend on the scheduler. Make sure the cron job from [Cron and queue](/installation/cron-and-queue) is running.
</Tip>
