MagicPost produces a PDF invoice for every completed order. The seller details come from **Finance** → **Invoice Settings**, the buyer details from checkout, and the amount from the order.

## Invoice settings

Open **Finance** → **Invoice Settings**. The page subtitle reads: configure your invoice preferences. Everything sits in one **Company Information** card.

| Field | Required | Notes |
|---|---|---|
| Company Name | Yes | Up to 255 characters. |
| Company Website | Yes | Must be a valid URL, for example one starting with https. |
| Business Address | Yes | Up to 255 characters. |
| City | Yes | Up to 100 characters. |
| State | No | Up to 100 characters. |
| Postal Code | No | Up to 20 characters. |
| Country | Yes | Pick from the searchable list. |
| Phone Number | No | Up to 30 characters. |
| VAT Number | No | Up to 50 characters. |

<Steps>
  <Step title="Fill in the card">
    Enter your company details. The placeholders show the kind of value each field expects.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**. The message **Settings were saved successfully** confirms it.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Until you save the form, the seller name on invoices falls back to the application name and the address block stays empty.

## How an invoice is built

| Part | Where it comes from |
|---|---|
| Invoice number | `INV.` followed by the order's internal number padded to five digits, for example `INV.00042`. It is stamped on the order when the order completes and never changes. |
| Date and status | The order's creation date and its status. |
| Seller | **Invoice Settings**. The company name, address, VAT number, phone and website. |
| Buyer | The name, email, phone and address collected at checkout. If the name or email are empty, the customer's account name and email are used. |
| Line item | The plan name, with its type and credit count, for example a Monthly plan with its credits. |
| Tax | The **Default Tax %** from [Finance settings](/saas/finance-settings). When it is above 0, the charged amount is treated as tax-inclusive and the invoice breaks it into net price, tax and total. The total always equals what the customer paid. |
| Footer notes | The order reference and the gateway used to pay. |
| Logo | The dashboard light logo, or the frontend logo when that is missing. |

Invoices are created on demand each time they are downloaded and are not stored as files. A change to **Invoice Settings** therefore shows on every invoice you or a customer download afterwards, including invoices for older orders. The order's number never changes.

<Note>
  The logo is only added when PHP's GD extension is available and the file is a PNG, JPEG, GIF or WebP image. Without a usable logo the invoice is still produced. Upload logos under [Website content](/admin/website-content).
</Note>

<Tip>
  To print the customer's address on invoices, switch **Billing Information** on in [Finance settings](/saas/finance-settings). Otherwise the buyer block shows only the account name and email.
</Tip>

## Download an invoice

Only completed orders have invoices. A pending, declined, cancelled or failed order has none.

**As an admin**

1. Go to **Finance** → **Orders**.
2. Open the row actions of a completed order and choose **Download Invoice**, or open the order and click **Invoice**.

**As a customer**

Customers download their own invoices with the **Download** button in **Billing history** on the **Billing** page, from the page they land on after paying, and with the download icon in the **Recent billing** card on their Profile page. See [Billing and subscription](/account/billing).

A customer can only download the invoices of their own orders. Admins can download any order's invoice.

## Free and manual orders

Free plan activations and orders you create with **Assign Subscription** are completed orders too, so they also get an invoice number and a downloadable invoice. A free activation shows an amount of 0.
