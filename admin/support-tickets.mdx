Customers open tickets from **Support** in their own menu, see [Support tickets](/account/support) for their side. You answer them under **Support**, **Tickets** in the admin sidebar. The page is titled **Support Tickets**: manage all customer support tickets.

## The queue

Four counters at the top show **Open Tickets**, **Pending Tickets**, **Resolved Tickets** and **Closed Tickets**. Below them is the table of tickets:

| Column | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Ticket ID** | The ticket's reference. |
| **Subject** | The first 30 characters of the subject. |
| **Priority** | Low, Medium, High or Urgent. |
| **Category** | The category the customer picked. |
| **Status** | Open, In Progress, Resolved or Closed. |
| **Created On** | Date and time the ticket was opened. |
| **Resolved On** | A resolution date, when one is recorded. Setting the status to Resolved in a reply does not fill it in. |

- Search by ticket ID, subject, priority, category or status. Click a heading to sort.
- Filter by **Status** (Open, In Progress, Resolved, Closed) or by **Category** (Technical, Billing, Account, General, Feature Request).
- Choose 10, 25, 50 or 100 rows per page. Your sort and search are remembered while you stay signed in.
- Tick several rows to delete them together with the bulk **Delete** action.
- Each row has a menu with **View** and **Delete**.

The [Admin dashboard](/admin/dashboard) also lists tickets that wait for a reply: open tickets where the customer wrote last.

## Reply to a ticket

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open the ticket">
    Click **View** in the row menu. The page is titled **Ticket ID** followed by the reference. It shows the **Ticket Subject**, **Ticket Status** and **Created By**, with the name and email of the customer. Below that is the whole conversation. Customer messages are marked **Customer Message** and yours **Support Team Response**. Attachments open from **View Attached Image**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Set the status">
    Under **Ticket Status** choose **Open**, **In Progress**, **Resolved** or **Closed**.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the reply">
    Type your answer under **Response**. It is required.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Attach an image (optional)">
    **Attach File** accepts JPG, JPEG or PNG up to 5 MB.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Send">
    Click **Reply**. The message is added to the conversation and the status you picked is applied in the same step.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Note>
  A reply shows up in the customer's ticket thread. This build does not send an email about it, so customers see the answer when they open the ticket.
</Note>

To close a ticket without writing back, set the status and reply with a short closing message. A reply is always required.

## Delete tickets

Use **Delete** in the row menu, or select rows and use the bulk action. Deleting is permanent. The ticket disappears from the customer's list as well.

## Tickets from one customer

On a customer's account page, the **Support Tickets** card lists their five latest tickets. See [Users](/admin/users).
