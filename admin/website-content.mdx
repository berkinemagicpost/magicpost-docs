Everything that visitors see before they sign in is managed under **Platform**, **Frontend Settings** in the admin sidebar. The group is collapsed by default, so click **Frontend Settings** to open it. Blog posts have their own page, see [Blog](/admin/blog).

| Page | Controls |
|---|---|
| **Frontend Settings** | Whether the landing page is public, a redirect to your own site, social links. |
| **SEO Manager** | Meta tags for the landing, login and registration pages. |
| **Logos** | Public site, dashboard and favicon images. |
| **Pages** | Privacy Policy and Terms of Service. |
| **FAQs Manager** | The FAQ section of the landing page. |
| **Testimonials** | The testimonials section of the landing page. |
| **Google Adsense** | Ad placements on the landing page and blog. |

Each page has its own **Save** button, except FAQs and Testimonials, which save from a window.

## Frontend Settings

The first item in the group is also called **Frontend Settings**. It is titled "Setup your frontend settings".

### Frontend Page

Switch **Frontend Page** controls the main landing page. It is on by default. When you turn it off, the landing page is not available to anyone: visitors who are not signed in are sent to the login screen and signed-in users go to their dashboard. Use it when MagicPost is a private tool and you do not want a marketing page.

### Custom Landing Page URL

Use this to send visitors to a marketing site that lives elsewhere.

1. Turn on the **Enable** switch under **Custom Landing Page URL**.
2. Enter the **Landing Page URL** as a full URL. The page notes that `https://` is required. The field is required while the switch is on.
3. Click **Save**.

Public pages then redirect to that address. A request is not redirected when the target is on the same host as your site, so it cannot loop.

<Warning>
  The redirect covers the whole public route group: the landing page, contact, Privacy Policy, Terms of Service, the blog and the social sign-in routes (`/auth/PROVIDER/redirect` and `/auth/PROVIDER/callback`). If you use social login from [Registration and login](/admin/registration-and-login), do not point the landing page to another domain unless you are sure those routes still work for you.
</Warning>

### Social Media Information

Optional links for **Twitter**, **Facebook**, **LinkedIn**, **Instagram**, **YouTube** and **TikTok**. Each must be a valid URL. The ones you fill in appear in the footer of the public site.

## SEO Manager

**SEO Manager** sets the meta tags for three pages: **SEO for Main Landing Page**, **SEO for Login Page** and **SEO for Registration Page**. Each has the same five fields:

| Field | Limit |
|---|---|
| Title (for example **Home Page Title**) | Up to 255 characters |
| Description | Free text |
| Keywords | Free text |
| Author | Up to 255 characters |
| Canonical URL | A valid URL, up to 255 characters |

All fields are optional. Blog posts have their own SEO fields, see [Blog](/admin/blog).

## Logos

Open **Logos**. Upload a file in each slot you want to change and click **Save**. A slot you leave empty keeps its current image.

| Slot | Used for | File |
|---|---|---|
| **Frontend Logo** | The wordmark on the public site, and the image shared in link previews. | Image, up to about 5 MB |
| **Frontend Collapsed Logo** | The compact mark on the public site. | Image, up to about 5 MB |
| **Favicon** | The browser tab icon. | `.ico` file, up to 1 MB |
| **Dashboard Light Logo** | The logo at the top of the sidebar on the light theme. | Image, up to about 5 MB |
| **Dashboard Dark Logo** | The logo at the top of the sidebar on the dark theme. | Image, up to about 5 MB |
| **Dashboard Collapsed Light Logo**, **Dashboard Collapsed Dark Logo** | Compact dashboard variants, stored the same way. | Image, up to about 5 MB |

Files are saved in `public/uploads/logo/`, named after the slot. If you upload no dashboard logo, the sidebar shows the initials of your site name instead. If you upload no favicon, the bundled `favicon.ico` is used.

<Tip>
  A PNG with a transparent background suits the dashboard logos. Upload a light version of the logo as the Dashboard Dark Logo so it stays readable on the dark sidebar.
</Tip>

## Pages

**Pages** holds the content of your **Privacy Policy** and **Terms of Service**, which are published at `/privacy` and `/terms`.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Choose the page">
    Click the **Privacy Policy** or **Terms of Service** tab.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Write the content">
    Enter text in the **Content** box. HTML is supported, for example `h2`, `p`, `ul`, `li` and `a` tags. Headings, paragraphs and lists are styled automatically. A **Live preview** shows the result, and **View page** opens the public page.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save Privacy Policy** or **Save Terms of Service**. Only the tab you are on is saved.
  </Step>
</Steps>

Leave a page empty to show the built-in default text. MagicPost records the date a page last changed and shows it on the public page.

<Note>
  Review the built-in text before you rely on it, and replace it with a policy that matches how you run your service.
</Note>

## FAQs Manager

**FAQs Manager** manages the questions shown in the FAQ section of the landing page.

- **Add FAQ** opens a window with **Question** (required, up to 255 characters), **Answer** (required) and **Status**.
- Only **Active** FAQs are shown on the landing page. **Inactive** ones are kept as drafts.
- Use **Search questions...** to filter. The list shows 10 per page, newest first.
- Each row has actions to edit and to delete the FAQ. Deleting asks for confirmation and is permanent.

## Testimonials

**Testimonials** manages the customer quotes on the landing page. **Add Testimonial** opens a window with:

| Field | Rule |
|---|---|
| **Testimonial** | Required, up to 1000 characters. |
| Stars | The rating, 1 to 5. The default is 5. |
| **Name** | Required, up to 255 characters. |
| **Role**, **Company** | Optional, up to 255 characters each. |
| **Avatar** | Optional. JPG or PNG, up to 2 MB. Without one, the initials of the name are used. **Remove avatar** deletes it. |
| **Featured** | Featured testimonials render as a full-black card to stand out. |
| **Status** | **Active** testimonials are shown, **Inactive** ones are hidden. |

Use the search box to find a testimonial by name, company or text. Avatars are saved in `public/uploads/testimonials/`.

## Google Adsense

**Google Adsense** shows ads on your public pages.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Get your publisher ID">
    In your AdSense account open **Account**, **Settings** and copy the publisher ID. It looks like `ca-pub-` followed by 16 digits.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Enable AdSense">
    Under **Account** turn on **Enable Google AdSense** and paste the **Publisher ID**. MagicPost refuses to save with AdSense enabled and no publisher ID.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Choose how ads are placed">
    **Auto Ads** lets Google place ads across your pages. You can still use the manual placements.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Add ad unit slots">
    Create an ad unit in your AdSense dashboard under **Ads**, **By ad unit**, copy its `data-ad-slot` value and paste it into the matching placement. Slot IDs are up to 30 characters. A placement with no slot ID does not render.
  </Step>
  <Step title="Save">
    Click **Save**.
  </Step>
</Steps>

The five placements are:

| Placement | Position |
|---|---|
| Home, Top | Below the hero on the landing page. |
| Home, Bottom | Above the footer on the landing page. |
| Blog, Top of listing | Top of the blog listing. |
| Blog post, In-article | Inside a blog post. |
| Blog post, Below content | Below a blog post. |

When **Enable Google AdSense** is off, no ad code is loaded on the public site.
