title: "System notifications"
description: "Read, clear and act on the in-app notifications your admin account receives, such as new sign-ups and completed payments."
---

**System Notifications** is the inbox of your admin account. Open it from **Notifications**, **System Notification** in the admin sidebar, or select **View all** in the Notifications card of the [Admin dashboard](/admin/dashboard). The page is titled **System Notifications**: manage system notifications.

It lists every notification your own account has received, 20 per page, newest first. Unread ones are highlighted.

## What arrives here

| Notification | Created when | Text |
|---|---|---|
| **New User Registered** | Someone signs up with the registration form. | Names the person and their email address. |
| **New Payment Received** | An order is completed (SaaS Business only). | Gives the order number, the amount and currency, and the gateway. |

Both go to the first admin account on the install, not to every admin. If you have more than one admin, only that account is notified. Each notification can carry a link, shown as **View**.

Any other notification your admin account receives, such as a publishing update for a post you scheduled yourself, also appears here, because this page lists the whole inbox of the signed-in account.

## Manage the list

- **View** opens the screen the notification points to.
- **Mark read** marks one notification as read.
- **Mark all as read** appears when there are unread items and clears them all.
- The trash icon deletes a notification after you confirm **Delete this notification?**.

An empty inbox reads **No notifications yet.**

<Note>
  **New User Registered** and **New Payment Received** are stored in the app only and are not emailed. Publishing notifications follow each account's own notification preferences, see [Notifications](/account/notifications).
</Note>
